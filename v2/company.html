The Catalyst for Change

The Attention Challenge

Working in a lab, I saw firsthand how switching between physical experiments and 2D instruction manuals was a constant source of friction and errors

The Idea

I knew AR technology could provide perfect spatial instructions for what I was accustomed to, but we needed a way to build these helpful tools without expensive, heavy equipment so anyone could use them

The Solution

The newest lightweight smart glasses gave us the everyday hardware we needed. LabCoat is the accessible software layer that makes those glasses useful, starting in the lab, but built for everywhere

Driven by Core Values

Our guiding principles

Human-Centered Design
