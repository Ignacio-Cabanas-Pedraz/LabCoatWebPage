Intuitive Platform to Manage Experiments

A comprehensive teacher-focused learning platform that puts you in control. Upload custom procedures, monitor student progress in real-time, and adapt experiments to your curriculum. Manage multiple laboratory sessions effortlessly with our intuitive dashboard.

Real-Time Student Monitoring

Track student progress live during laboratory sessions. See which steps students are on, identify who needs help, and intervene proactively to ensure successful learning outcomes.

Intelligent, Adaptive Architecture

LabCoat AI is built on a modular, deterministic architecture designed to eliminate hallucinations and provide reliable, accurate guidance. Our system combines specialized AI reasoning with augmented reality for seamless laboratory learning.

Deterministic, Not Probabilistic

Fixed reasoning paths ensure consistent, reliable responses without the unpredictability of traditional AI models.

Modular & Customizable

Compartmentalized components adapt to your institution's specific equipment, procedures, and teaching methodology.

Multi-Workstation Spatial Context

Advanced spatial awareness allows LabCoat AI to remember and navigate multiple laboratory environments simultaneously.
