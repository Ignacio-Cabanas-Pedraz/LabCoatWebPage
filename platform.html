02 / Edge Computer Vision

Contextual Vision Without Surveillance Overhead

By recognizing equipment asset tags, reagent barcodes, and container QR codes passively through the camera lens, the scientist never has to aim a handheld scanner or manual barcode reader again.

  • ✓
    Passive Asset Tag Detection: Looking at a biosafety hood automatically loads its current certification, airflow status, and active protocol.
  • ✓
    Automatic Lot Verification: Picking up a bottle validates expiration dates and lot numbers against active master batch records in real time.
  • ✓
    Zero Operator Privacy Compromise: Raw video is never continuously transmitted or stored. Only verified protocol milestone clips are retained for audit replay.
03 / Zero-Latency Edge Engine

Embedded WebAssembly PGlite for Faraday-Grade Reliability

Regulated cleanrooms are heavy physical enclosures shielded by stainless steel and airlocks that frequently kill Wi-Fi. Traditional cloud-only SaaS apps stall with loading spinners or drop critical records.

  • ✓
    100% Offline Capability: The complete relational database and validation schema run directly on the edge using embedded PostgreSQL via WebAssembly (PGlite).
  • ✓
    Cryptographic Time-Lock: Records created offline receive NTP-synchronized local timestamps and SHA-256 cryptographic hashes that prevent backdating.
  • ✓
    Zero-Loss Sync Gateway: When the technician exits into a connected gowning room or corridor, data synchronizes to the central server in sub-second batches.
