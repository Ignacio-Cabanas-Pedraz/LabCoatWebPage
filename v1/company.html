The Moment Everything Changed

The Attention Challenge

I spent countless hours puzzled in the laboratory, realizing that a single teacher for 10-12 students simply isn't enough. Without immediate guidance, students struggle to master complex procedures, leading to frustration rather than learning.

The Inspiration

I witnessed AR's potential in surgery aided by HoloLens, but I was paralyzed by the prohibitive hardware costs ($3,500+). There was simply no way to bring this technology to students at scale.

The Breakthrough

Then, almost overnight, the barrier was removed. Meta introduced affordable smart glasses with high-quality displays and AI integration. Finally, we had the hardware to build a hands-free laboratory teacher for everyone.

We can now implement affordable solutions to increase productivity in many fields, especially scientific research.
