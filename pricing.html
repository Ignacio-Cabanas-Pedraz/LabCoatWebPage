Procurement & Validation Assurance

How does hardware procurement work for smart glasses?

LabCoat software runs on enterprise-certified lightweight smart glasses (including Vuzix, RealWear, and Meta Ray-Ban Enterprise). You can either bring your own qualified hardware or procure turnkey pre-provisioned glasses through our hardware fulfillment partners.

What is included in the GAMP 5 CSV Validation Package?

The Cleanroom Pro and Enterprise packages include standard Installation Qualification (IQ), Operational Qualification (OQ), and Performance Qualification (PQ) executed protocol templates along with our 21 CFR Part 11 Traceability Matrix to fast-track internal QA signoff.

Can we host LabCoat on-premise or in an isolated VPC?

Yes. For Global Enterprise clients with strict sovereign data residency or air-gapped facility requirements, we support self-contained Kubernetes clusters deployed inside your private AWS/Azure/GCP VPC or local server racks.

How does LabCoat protect data integrity if Wi-Fi drops?

Our architecture uses embedded PGlite on the edge device. Every observation, barcode scan, and timestamp is cryptographically hashed and stored locally first. Once network connectivity re-establishes, the local ledger synchronizes via conflict-free consensus.
