Phased Implementation

The 4-Week Rollout Blueprint

A structured, low-risk roadmap managed collaboratively between your QA/Operations teams and LabCoat Solutions Engineers.

WEEK 01
Facility & SOP Ingestion
Scoping & Mapping

Digital twin mapping and Master Batch Record (MBR) digitization.

  • Spatial scan of biosafety cabinets, incubators, cold storage, and gowning airlocks.
  • Ingestion of core Standard Operating Procedures into structured, step-by-step guidance models.
  • Asset tag generation and QR pairing for all target instruments and reagent shelving.
WEEK 02
Hardware & Edge Gateway
Infrastructure Setup

Fleet staging, local edge hub installation, and LIMS connector configuration.

  • Provisioning of wearable AR smart glasses with custom prescription lens inserts as needed.
  • Deployment of the on-premises or private VPC Edge Gateway and offline PGlite synchronization nodes.
  • Configuration of SSO / SAML 2.0 and bidirectional API webhooks with your LIMS/ELN instance.
WEEK 03
GAMP 5 IQ/OQ Execution
QA Qualification

Formal validation package execution alongside your Quality Assurance department.

  • Installation Qualification (IQ) scripts executed to verify hardware, encryption, and system boundaries.
  • Operational Qualification (OQ) tests executed for 21 CFR Part 11 e-signatures and tamper-evident audit trails.
  • QA sign-off on the Requirements Traceability Matrix (RTM) and system risk assessments.
WEEK 04
Operator Training & Go-Live
Production Cutover

Technician certification, parallel shadow runs, and official commercial batch cutover.

  • Hands-on training session (under 4 hours per technician shift) covering voice and wristband controls.
  • First live batch execution under LabCoat guidance with dedicated on-site support engineers.
  • Official transition to 100% paperless, contemporaneous batch records.
