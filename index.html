Step-by-Step Procedure Guidance

Navigate complex laboratory procedures with confidence. LabCoat AI provides real-time, personalized guidance through your Meta Ray-Ban smart glasses, adapting to your pace and responding to your questions as you work.

Automatic Lab Notebook Generation

Focus on learning, not note-taking. LabCoat AI automatically documents your procedures, observations, and results, creating comprehensive lab reports ready for submission.

Procedural Error Catching & Safety

Stay safe and avoid mistakes. Real-time error detection warns you of procedural deviations and safety hazards before they become problems, acting as your personal safety supervisor.

Laboratory Equipment Library

Access comprehensive information on lab equipment and proper usage techniques. Learn the correct operation of instruments and understand laboratory etiquette for professional development.

Intelligent, Adaptive Architecture

LabCoat AI is built on a modular, deterministic architecture designed to eliminate hallucinations and provide reliable, accurate guidance. Our system combines specialized AI reasoning with augmented reality for seamless laboratory learning.

Deterministic, Not Probabilistic

Fixed reasoning paths ensure consistent, reliable responses without the unpredictability of traditional AI models.

Modular & Customizable

Compartmentalized components adapt to your institution's specific equipment, procedures, and teaching methodology.

Multi-Workstation Spatial Context

Advanced spatial awareness allows LabCoat AI to remember and navigate multiple laboratory environments simultaneously.
