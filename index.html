How It Works

Automated Data Capture

Wearable AR glasses automatically record every action, equipment usage, and compound movement.

Immutable Audit Trails

Events are stored as an immutable, event-sourced log in a centralized database, ensuring a tamper-proof record.

Salesforce-Like Dashboard

A comprehensive web dashboard provides real-time visibility, reporting, and actionable analytics.

Real-Time LIMS Integration

Bi-directional synchronization instantly updates existing Laboratory Inventory Management Systems (LIMS).

Integrations & Features

LIMS Integration

Instantly synchronize with your Laboratory Information Management System. Automate inventory updates and monitor equipment status in real-time without manual intervention.

AI-Enhanced Insights

Leverage intelligent pattern detection to extract actionable efficiency metrics. Receive automated anomaly alerts and usage optimizations for your laboratory staff.

Secure Video Evidence

Highly compressed video recordings are securely stored in AWS S3 and linked to individual audit events for full traceability.

Compliance & Security

Engineered to meet GDPR, HIPAA, and FDA electronic record requirements from day 1 with audit-proof logs and AES-256 encryption.

Unmatched Ease of Use

Experience 100% hands-free interaction via smart glasses, supported by local-first offline capability ensuring no data is ever lost.

Built for Enterprise Leaders

Empower your management and IT teams with secure, scalable compliance tools.

Laboratory Managers

Eliminate manual paperwork and signature bottlenecks. Gain instant, searchable audit logs that provide complete visibility into inventory and equipment usage, reducing audit preparation time by up to 70%.

CIOs & IT Leaders

Deploy a secure, cloud-native SaaS solution that integrates with existing systems via REST APIs. Our modular Go and PostgreSQL architecture scales horizontally with comprehensive monitoring via Prometheus and Grafana.

Technical Excellence

Built on a modular, event-sourced architecture designed for high performance, immutability, and enterprise-grade reliability.

High-Performance Backend

Powered by a Go modular monolith and ElectricSQL for seamless real-time data synchronization between the AR client and cloud.

Advanced Security Stack

AES-256 encryption at rest, TLS 1.3 in transit, and event sourcing ensure that every log entry is immutable and audit-proof.

Scalability & Reliability

Containerized deployments via Docker/Kubernetes on AWS with integrated Sentry, Prometheus, and Grafana for 99.99% uptime.
